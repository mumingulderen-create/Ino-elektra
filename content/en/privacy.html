Privacy statement

Brief and clear: which data and cookies we use, why, from which providers and for how long. This is a translation; the Dutch version is the official one.

Who are we?

INO Techniek en Installatie, based in Utrecht. Questions about privacy? Email {{email}} or call {{tel}}.

Which data do we use?

Only what you give us yourself: name, phone number, email address, the address of the job, your description and any photos. We use this data solely to answer your request, carry out the job and send the invoice.

Forms

When you send a form, your request goes over a secure connection to our own processing service at Cloudflare. It checks the request and forwards it via the email service Brevo to our mailbox. If you entered an email address, you receive a short confirmation from us by email. Your request and photos are not stored along the way; they only end up in our mailbox. We automatically remove location data (GPS) from photos.

To prevent spam and abuse we use Cloudflare Turnstile. It checks that a human is filling in the form, without advertising or tracking cookies. To limit abuse, your IP address is briefly used to count the number of submissions per minute; we do not store it.

WhatsApp messages go through WhatsApp (Meta). We never sell or share your data with other parties for marketing.

How long do we keep your data?

Requests that do not lead to a job are deleted within 12 months. We keep invoices and related data for 7 years, because Dutch law (the tax retention obligation) requires this.

Cookies, Google Consent Mode v2 and consent

In the cookie banner you can choose separately by category: Necessary storage (always on), Google Analytics (statistics) and Google Ads (conversion tracking). Optional categories are never pre-ticked and rejecting ("Necessary only") is just as easy as accepting.

We implement Google Consent Mode v2. By default, all optional storage and ad signals (analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization) are set to denied. Before you give explicit consent, no Google scripts are loaded and no requests are sent to Google Analytics or Google Ads. The font is hosted on our own server, so nothing is sent to Google for it.

  • Google Analytics 4 (G-HR6L1S8V7P) — provider: Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. Measures anonymously which pages are viewed and how often call, WhatsApp or quote buttons are used (analytics_storage is set to granted only with this choice).
  • Google Ads (AW-7124503256) — provider: Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. Measures whether a click on our Google advert leads to a phone call, WhatsApp message or submitted form (conversion_event_contact). Only when you consent to Google Ads conversion tracking are ad_storage and ad_user_data (for measuring and sharing conversion data) set to granted. ad_personalization always remains denied: we do not run personalised ads or remarketing and do not sell data.

Retention period and withdrawing consent: your cookie choice is stored in your browser for up to 12 months (365 days). After 12 months, any previous consent expires automatically and we will not load Google Analytics or Google Ads until you make a new choice. You can change or withdraw your preferences at any time via Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw consent, future tracking stops immediately and we delete previously placed first-party tracking cookies (such as _ga*, _gid, _gat*, _gcl_* and _gac_*) where technically possible. You can also clear cookies manually in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can always ask which data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Send us an email and we will arrange it within 30 days. If you disagree with something, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
